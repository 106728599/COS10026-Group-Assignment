Reference Number: TRV26

Job Title: Data Engineer

Building and maintaining data pipelines that turn all flight, hotel, and booking events into insights for pricing, marketing, and operations teams

Salary: $4000-$8000 per month + Performance Bonus

Reporting to: Lead Data Engineer and Head of Data & Analytics

Responsibilities

  1. Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines to process large volumes of data from various sources.
  2. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data requirements and deliver solutions that meet business needs.
  3. Implement data quality checks and monitoring to ensure the accuracy and reliability of data.
  4. Optimize data storage and retrieval processes for performance and cost efficiency.
  5. Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies and best practices in data engineering.

Essential Requirements

  1. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
  2. Proven experience as a Data Engineer or similar role, with a strong understanding of data pipelines and ETL processes.
  3. Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or Scala.
  4. Experience with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) and cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure).
  5. Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently and in a team environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  1. Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
  2. Experience with machine learning and artificial intelligence technologies.

Job Title: IT Support Specialist

Providing technical support and assistance to end-users, troubleshooting hardware and software issues, and ensuring the smooth operation of IT systems within the organization.

Salary: $3000-$5000 per month + Performance Bonus

Reporting to: IT Manager

Responsibilities

  1. Respond to user inquiries and provide technical support via phone, email, or in-person.
  2. Troubleshoot hardware and software issues, including desktops, laptops, printers, and other peripherals.
  3. Install, configure, and maintain computer systems and software applications.
  4. Monitor and maintain IT infrastructure, including servers, networks, and security systems.
  5. Document support activities and maintain accurate records of issues and resolutions.

Essential Requirements

  1. Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
  2. Proven experience as an IT Support Specialist or similar role.
  3. Strong knowledge of computer systems, networks, and software applications.
  4. Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
  5. Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Preferred Qualifications

  1. Certifications such as CompTIA A+, Microsoft Certified IT Professional (MCITP), or Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA).
  2. Experience with cloud-based technologies and virtualization.
